Linguistics

Research Areas

My research in linguistics spans several key areas, from theoretical foundations to practical applications in computational linguistics and natural language processing.

Theoretical Linguistics

Investigating fundamental principles of language structure, syntax, and semantics. Focus on universal grammar and cross-linguistic variations.

Computational Linguistics

Applying computational methods to linguistic analysis, including machine learning approaches to language processing and understanding.

Research & Development

Current and ongoing R&D projects that bridge theoretical linguistics with practical applications.

Natural Language Processing Tools

Development of advanced NLP algorithms and tools for text analysis, sentiment analysis, and language understanding.

Language Learning Technologies

Creating innovative technologies and methodologies to enhance language learning and acquisition processes.

Corpus Linguistics

Building and analyzing large-scale language corpora to understand patterns of language use and variation.

Cross-linguistic Analysis

Comparative studies across different language families to identify universal patterns and unique characteristics.
