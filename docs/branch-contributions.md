## Bringing other branches in

Each branch must be able to supply its own people, sources and proposed relationships. A catalogue seed neither appoints a branch head nor assigns authority. For each contribution retain:

- full contributor name and the branch claimed; review branch membership separately from provenance;
- source title, edition, printed page, URL or record reference, original image if approved, and transcription;
- exact full names and identifiers for each person; dates/places with uncertainty;
- one explicit relationship or title claim per statement, linked to its supporting passage;
- public/private sharing scope and attributable review/correction history.

Do not merge people on surname alone or use a title succession as a parent–child relationship. Keep incoming statements as proposed until reviewed; contradictory contributions coexist with their evidence. JEV can assess explicit named candidate connections against supplied passages, with its assessment recorded separately from accepted genealogy. This title catalogue itself cannot establish a person-to-person connection.
