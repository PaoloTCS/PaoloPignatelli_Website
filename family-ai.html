01 · A real research example

From Montecalvo toward Lucio

Recorded AI-assisted investigation · 4 October 2026

Explore what our first investigation found, the sources it used, and where it must stop. This is a replay of existing findings, not a live search or independently running agents.

The paternal line

Read upward into the past. Select a person to inspect the relationship to the next generation.

    Unresolved medieval connection

    Lucio Pignatelli · traditionally placed in 1102

    No verified chain connects Tommaso to Lucio in this investigation. The identification of Lucio itself is disputed. This gap is deliberately left open.

    Inspect the evidence

    What the labels mean

    Family testimony: a named relative confirms the relationship.

    Published genealogy: a compilation reports the link; original records still need checking.

    Disputed: sources disagree. Repetition is not independent corroboration.

    How agent work can be organized

    Explorer, reviewer, coordinator

    Roles in a proposed workflow
    Sources and scope of this investigation
    1. Ronald Elward · The Heirs of Europe: Pignatelli di Montecalvo

      Published recent ancestry. An entry under Paolo’s name includes a birth-date correction; dates should be checked rather than copied unquestioningly.

    2. Genmarenostrum · Duchi di Montecalvo

      Earlier Montecalvo relationships, consulted through indexed excerpts. Original records were not examined.

    3. Genmarenostrum · Marchesi di Casalnuovo

      The published Stefano–Cesare–Annibale connection, consulted through indexed excerpts.

    4. Pignatelli della Leonessa · Storia della Famiglia

      Traditional Lucio account and the fifteenth-century division associated with Tommaso’s sons Stefano, Carlo, and Palamede. Montecalvo is placed in Stefano’s line.

    5. Lucia Lopriore · Review of Davide Shamà’s 2009 Pignatelli study

      Challenges Lucio’s historical documentation; describes uncertain early generations. We read the review, not the underlying book or archival documents.

    6. Paolo Pignatelli · Family testimony, 4 October 2026.

      Confirmed Guido as his father and Pompeo as his grandfather. This confirms those two relationships, not every earlier generation.

    02 · Useful work to propose

    What should we investigate next?

    These are proposed errands derived from gaps in the first investigation. No request has been sent and no task has been assigned.

    Competing historical accounts

    What supports the Lucio tradition?

    Compare the traditional genealogy with Shamà’s study. Locate the cited documents and distinguish a contemporary record from a later family account.

    Useful contribution: a book page, archival reference, or legible scan with its source.

    Original records needed

    Check the Montecalvo links

    Start with one published parent–child relationship and locate the record supporting it. Keep ancestry separate from succession to a title.

    Useful contribution: a civil, parish, marriage, or family archival record.

    Connect the branches

    Compare our branch histories

    Ask each branch head to assemble a sourced path to its earliest secure ancestor. Compare shared people before trying to join the paths.

    Useful contribution: a reviewed list of relationships and evidence from another branch.

    03 · Try it with your own branch

    Give your agent a clear assignment.

    Choose a starting point and generate instructions to paste into ChatGPT or an agent with browsing. The prompt asks for evidence and returns a useful result even when the path is incomplete.

    This form generates text on your device. It does not send family information, start an agent, or store your entries.

    Bring back something the family can use.

    1. Paste the instructions into your chosen AI assistant and provide only material you may share.
    2. Ask it to browse and cite sources. If a source cannot be accessed, keep that limitation visible.
    3. Review each proposed relationship. Ask it to separate records, family testimony, and hypotheses.
    4. Share a short result with your cousins: what was found, what remains open, and one useful next errand.

    For now, contributions are coordinated through the family’s existing WhatsApp conversations. This page has no WhatsApp integration, submission service, or access to the family database.

    04 · The larger purpose

    Family connections, made active.

    The genealogy gives context. The larger ambition is to help cousins find shared interests, exchange knowledge, plan visits, and build things together.

    What we want agents to help with

    • Find a cousin who shares an interest or knows a particular story.
    • Translate a letter or preserve a relative’s voice.
    • Suggest a joint errand when different branches hold different pieces.
    • Prepare an introduction that the people involved can choose to accept.

    These connection services are planned, not active. Members would decide what to share and who may see it; introductions would require their agreement.
